Rule 1: No cheating

Players must complete levels without using any cheats or exploits, including in the verification process.

Allowed MegaHack/QOL Mods

The following mods are allowed:

  • Show Hitboxes on Death
  • Any mods that do not affect the gameplay or create an unfair advantage

Level Requirements

  • At least 1 minute of active gameplay
  • Must be verified(Proof required)
  • At least Simple Decoration
  • If going for top 3 level needs different requirements
  • Old Style Decoration allowed
  • Cannot be a level preview
  • Must have good gameplay that is fun to play, and is skill-based
  • Learny gameplay is allowed as long as some skill-based gameplay is present
  • Layouts CAN be added to the list if the gameplay is good, fun, and meets the quality standards
  • If your level has too much learny gameplay, it will not be accepted, as we want more skill than levels that you have to learn

Top 3 Requirements

  • At least 1 minute 30 seconds of active gameplay
  • Better Decoration(Actually Eye pleasing decoration)
  • Old Style Decoration allowed

Old Style Decoration

  • Old Style Decoration MUST look like an old RobTob level to get added
  • If going for a top 3 then you need at least 10-15 active Group IDs depending on length of the level

Group ID Requirements

  • Long: 10
  • XL: 12-15
  • XL(2:00): 12
  • XL(2:30): 13
  • XL(3:00+): 15
